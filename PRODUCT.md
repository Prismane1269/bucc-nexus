# BUCC NEXUS — Product Truth (PRODUCT.md)

## 1. Overview
**BUCC NEXUS: BUILD WHAT'S NEXT.** is the premier 48-hour student innovation hackathon organized by the **BRAC University Computer Club (BUCC)** under the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, BRAC University.

## 2. Event Core Facts
- **Format**: 48 continuous building hours (Hybrid: Physical workstation access in BRAC University campus laboratories with 24/7 technical mentorship).
- **Participants**: 300+ undergraduate student builders from BRAC University.
- **Team Size**: 2 to 4 members per team (interdisciplinary collaboration encouraged across CSE, EEE, Architecture, and Business).
- **Total Prize Pool**: 50,000+ BDT alongside physical trophies, certificates of merit, incubation support, and BUCC Hall of Fame honors.
- **Official Domains & Email**: Participant verification through official student accounts (`@g.bracu.ac.bd`).

## 3. The 4 Innovation Tracks
1. **AI & Data Science**: Autonomous agent workflows, multimodal models, computer vision, and predictive analytics.
2. **Web & App Development**: High-performance, distributed, accessible web/mobile applications with real-time state.
3. **Robotics & IoT**: Embedded hardware, microcontrollers (ESP32, Raspberry Pi), edge sensors, and autonomous systems.
4. **Open Innovation**: Decentralized protocols, cybersecurity, assistive technologies, and civic tools.
