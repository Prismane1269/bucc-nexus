# BUCC NEXUS — Design System & Visual World (DESIGN.md)
*Crafted in accordance with Framer & Awwwards SOTD Design Direction*

## 1. Design Thesis & Creative Direction
BUCC NEXUS embodies a monumental, high-performance dark aesthetic engineered for elite student builders, engineers, and designers. Rejecting generic AI templates, purple gradient fills, and repetitive card grids, the interface is structured around:
- **Obsidian Dark Canvas**: Deepest charcoal `#05070A` foundation accented by subtle radial ambient glows and crisp hairline micro-borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Asymmetrical Bento Composition**: Content flows through varied, intentional spatial rhythms rather than uniform boxes.
- **Monumental Typography**: Confident display scales with tight negative tracking (`-0.035em`) prioritizing legibility, hierarchy, and authentic craft.
- **Living 3D & Video Telemetry**: Interactive Three.js WebGL particle convergence synced with 4 high-definition video streams.

- **Secondary Technical Accent (Icy Cyan)**: `#00F0FF` / `#6EE7FF` (Glow: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`)
- **Text Primary (Pure High-Contrast)**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary (Titanium Slate)**: `#94A3B8`
- **Text Muted**: `#64748B`
- **Hairline Border**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Hairline Border Focus/Hover**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Headlines**: `Syne`, sans-serif (700, 800 weight, letter-spacing `-0.03em`, line-height `1.05`)
- **Section Titles**: `Syne`, sans-serif (700 weight, letter-spacing `-0.02em`)
- **Body & Microcopy**: `Sora`, sans-serif (300, 400, 500, 600 weights, line-height `1.6`)
- **Data, Badges & Code**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (500, 600 weights, uppercase tracking `0.12em`)

---

## 4. Layout Architecture & Sections
1. **Floating Glass Pill Header**: Fixed elevated pill with frosted glass blur (`24px`), brand mark, nav anchors, and shimmer registration CTA.
2. **Monumental Hero Stage**: High-impact headline, live 72-hour countdown pill, dual primary/secondary action buttons, and a 3D WebGL portal workstation with live multi-camera telemetry switcher.
3. **Tech Ecosystem Matrix Grid**: Curated 4-column toolchain showcase (Machine Intelligence, Web & 3D Interactive, Embedded & Robotics, Systems & Infrastructure).
4. **Impact Metrics Banner**: High-contrast, clean statistics (48 Hrs, 4 Tracks, 50K+ BDT, 300+ Hackers).
5. **Asymmetrical Innovation Bento Grid**:
   - Featured interactive Track Sandbox with real-time architecture blueprints and starter code.
   - Interactive track selector cards with dynamic cursor spotlight.
6. **48-Hour Sprint Journey**: Interactive tabbed timeline with day pills and category filters (Keynotes, Hacking, Mentorship, Meals).
7. **Prestige Prize Podium**: 3-tier metallic podium (Champion with gold sheen, First Runner Up with silver sheen, Second Runner Up with bronze sheen) plus special category grants.
8. **Interactive Registration Portal**: Domain-verified intake (`@g.bracu.ac.bd`), team size selector (2–4 members), and real-time vision pitch counter.
9. **FAQ Accordion with Live Search**: Instant filtering and silky spring expansion.
10. **Floating Glass Quick Dock**: Bottom floating capsule with countdown timer, audio SFX toggle, and quick anchors.
11. **Official Editorial Footer**: BUCC crest, BRAC University CSE affiliation, and verified social links.
