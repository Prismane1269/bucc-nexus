## ⚡ Core Features

- **12 Comprehensive Sections**:
  1. Fixed Glassmorphic Navigation with Official BUCC & BRAC University Crests
  2. Hero Section with Live 48-Hour Sprint Countdown & Dual CTAs
  3. Key Hackathon Metrics Banner
  4. About BUCC NEXUS & 4 Core Values (*Community, Creativity, Collaboration, Growth*)
  5. 4 Innovation Tracks (*AI & Data Science, Web & App Development, Robotics & IoT, Open Innovation*)
  6. 48-Hour Interactive Schedule Timeline with Day Tabs
  7. Supporting Experiences & Hardware Lab Preview
  8. Challenge & Judging Evaluation Framework (*30% Tech, 25% Innovation, 25% Impact, 20% Design*)
  9. Prizes & Podium Honors (*50,000+ BDT Total Pool*)
  10. Priority Registration Form with BRACU Domain Validation (`@g.bracu.ac.bd`)
  11. Interactive FAQ Accordion
  12. Official Footer with CSE Department Affiliation and Campus HQ

- **Signature 3D & Video Suite**:
  - **Three.js WebGL Engine**: Zero-GC render loop, pre-allocated scratch vectors, adaptive DPR clamping `[1, 1.5]`, pointer-events pass-through.
  - **4 Synchronized Video Streams**:
    - `hero-energy-trails.mp4`: Converging energy pathways in dark architecture
    - `nexus-geometric-ring.mp4`: Rotating holographic 3D gateway ring
    - `network-nodes.mp4`: Interconnected campus neural network
    - `futuristic-tech.mp4`: Rapid hardware prototyping & edge computing
  - **Interactive 3D Telemetry Switcher**: Real-time camera feed switcher between all 4 streams.
