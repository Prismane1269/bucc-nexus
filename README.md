# BUCC NEXUS — BUILD WHAT'S NEXT.

Official landing page and registration portal for **BUCC NEXUS**, a 48-hour student innovation hackathon organized by the **BRAC University Computer Club (BUCC)**.

🌐 **Website**: [https://prismane1269.github.io/bucc-nexus/](https://prismane1269.github.io/bucc-nexus/)

---

## About the Event

BUCC NEXUS brings together student developers, designers, and innovators to collaborate, build, and showcase functional technology prototypes across specialized tracks including Artificial Intelligence, Web & Mobile Applications, and Robotics & IoT.

---

## Website Features

- **Event Overview & Details**: Information about the hackathon tracks, venue, and format.
- **Registration Countdown**: Live tracking for participant registration deadlines.
- **Event Timeline**: Overview of the 48-hour hackathon schedule and milestones.
- **Team Registration Portal**: Online registration and interest intake for student teams.
- **Rules & FAQ**: Guidelines, eligibility criteria, and frequently asked questions.
